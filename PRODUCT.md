# Product

## Register

product

## Users
General social users: people at parties, meetups, classes, conventions and other social events who want a quiet way to show how open they are to being approached. They glance at the app in a crowd, often one-handed, often in mixed light, and usually mid-conversation or about to walk into one. A paired Wear OS watch mirrors their status.

## Product Purpose
Unspoken Cues lets someone set one of four statuses (Green: open to connect, Yellow: proceed with care, Red: please give me space, Purple: maybe open to connect) and have it seen by others without saying a word. Around that core: events people host or join with a code, a profile with preferences and boundaries, and S.W.A.P. cards that people collect into a binder by scanning each other's QR code. Success is a user changing their cue in under two seconds and other people reading it correctly at a glance.

## Brand Personality
Playful, warm, social. Friendly and a little fun (card collecting, swapping), never cold or clinical. The status colors are the hero; everything else stays calm around them so they read instantly.

## Anti-references
- Clinical health or mental-health apps (white + teal, sterile forms).
- Glossy dating apps: swipe stacks, gradient-heavy CTAs, pushy engagement.
- Generic SaaS dashboards: identical card grids, hero metrics, side-stripe accents.

## Design Principles
1. The cue is the hero. The current status is the largest, clearest thing wherever it appears.
2. Never color alone. Every status color is paired with its name or meaning so color-blind users read it the same way.
3. Glanceable in a crowd. Big targets, short copy, one obvious action per screen.
4. Warm, not loud. Personality comes from tone and small moments, not from extra color or motion.
5. Respect boundaries in the UI itself. Privacy and visibility choices are plain and never buried.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA contrast for all text and controls, including text on status colors.
- Color-blind safe: status is never conveyed by color alone.
- Reduced motion / low sensory: motion only to show state change, short and calm; nothing flashing.
- Touch targets at least 48dp.
